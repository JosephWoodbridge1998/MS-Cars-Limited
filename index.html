Who we are

About MS Cars Limited

MS Cars Limited is a used car dealership based in Reading, Berkshire, built around a simple idea: buying a used car should be straightforward, honest and tailored to you.

We carefully select every vehicle that comes onto our forecourt to make sure it meets our standards for quality, performance and value — whether you're after a first car, a family runaround, or something a little more refined.

Our team is on hand to give honest advice, transparent pricing and a hassle-free buying process from your first enquiry to the moment you drive away.

AA Cars trading standards

As a dealer that follows the AA Cars trading standards, we build every sale around a few core commitments:

  • Checked before sale. Vehicles are inspected before they're offered for sale on our forecourt.

  • Honest, transparent pricing. No hidden extras — the price you see is the price you're quoted.

  • Clear paperwork. Full details on any finance or part-exchange enquiry, in plain English.

  • A dealership you can raise concerns with. Straightforward aftercare if something isn't right.

Summary of our approach to the AA Cars code of practice — full certificate and terms available in branch on request.
